## 1. Data we collect

The developer **does not request, collect, or store** personal user data (name, e-mail, phone number, address, payment information, etc.) on its own servers.

The Application **does not require** registration or sign-in.

However, while the Application is running, the following technical data may be automatically collected and processed by **third-party services** (see Section 3):

- device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID);
- device model, operating system version, language, region;
- IP address;
- information about interaction with advertisements (impressions, clicks);
- aggregated crash and performance statistics.

This data is used solely in anonymized form and does not allow direct identification of the user.

## 2. Purposes of data processing

Technical data collected by third-party services is used to:

- display advertisements (including targeted ones) within the Application;
- prevent fraud and abuse;
- analyze stability and improve the Application;
- comply with legal requirements.

The user has the right to:
